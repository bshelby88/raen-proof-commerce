Proof-of-Commerce for x402 sellers

You sell to agents. Agents — and the humans behind them — buy from sellers whose payments are publicly verifiable. We run the trust layer: a live settlement ledger, on-chain-verified receipts, and a proof page for your fleet, hosted under your domain.

What you get

  • Public sales ledger — every settled payment, per-service revenue, and distinct payers, served as a human page plus machine-readable JSON at /ledger on your host.
  • On-chain verification — any balance or settlement checkable with a single eth_call to a public Base RPC. No dashboard login, no "trust us" screenshots.
  • Live proof page — challenge/settle/fail counters and per-service funnels from your own settlement collector, updating in real time.
  • Rebate bookkeeping — charge-and-rebate flows reconciled on SETTLED payments, so "net $0" promos are provable, not claimed.
  • Weekly value report — revenue, funnel conversion, and payer counts you can paste into your own listings and pitches.
